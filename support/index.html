Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone/iPad: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Love Piano → Cancel Subscription.
Android: open Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Love Piano → Cancel.
Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the App does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or in your Google Play order history.

I bought Premium but it is locked on my new device.

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase. Then open the paywall in the App and tap Restore purchases.

My MIDI keyboard is not detected.

Connect the keyboard before you open a lesson. For USB, use a USB-C or Lightning adapter that supports MIDI devices. For Bluetooth MIDI, turn on Bluetooth and put the keyboard in pairing mode.

The microphone does not hear my notes.

Allow microphone access in your device settings. Put the phone near the piano, in a quiet room. Play each note clearly. Microphone audio is processed on your device and is never recorded.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Settings → Privacy → Delete account in the App. This permanently removes your account and progress. You can also email us to ask for deletion. See the Privacy Policy for details.
