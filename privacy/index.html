Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Quang DN, an individual developer ("we", "us", "our"), operates the Love Piano mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Account data (optional). If you choose to link a Google or Apple account (Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple), we receive your name and email address from that provider. You can use the App anonymously without any personal information.

1.3 Microphone (Optional)

When you choose microphone input, the App captures audio in real time only to detect which piano notes you play. Audio is processed on your device. It is never stored, transmitted, or uploaded. You can revoke microphone permission in your device settings at any time. The App still works with the on-screen keyboard or MIDI input.

1.4 MIDI Device Data (Optional)

When you connect a MIDI controller, the App reads MIDI note events over USB or Bluetooth. MIDI data is processed on your device only and is never transmitted.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Account and progress data is kept while your account exists. To delete it, open Settings → Privacy → Delete account in the App. This permanently removes your account and progress data, at the latest within 30 days.
  • Linked account data (Google or Apple email) is kept until account deletion.
  • Analytics events are kept for 14 months.
  • Crash logs are kept for 90 days.

You can also ask for deletion by email at ngocquangbbb@gmail.com.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP on personal data protection), you have the right to:

  • Know how we collect and process your personal data.
  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data.
  • Restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email ngocquangbbb@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 gave us personal data, we delete it promptly. If you think this happened, contact ngocquangbbb@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

  • All data between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • Backend data is protected by access controls and encrypted storage provided by Google Cloud.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If you find a security issue, report it to ngocquangbbb@gmail.com.

8. International Data Transfers

Our backend runs on Google Cloud in Singapore (asia-southeast1). Google may also process analytics and crash data in other countries. If you are in the EU/EEA or Vietnam, your data may be transferred outside your country. Google applies safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the App shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, no advertising-related data is collected or shared. You can change your choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. If a change is material, we tell you in the App at least 14 days before it takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
